# DSA instruction is outsourced to NeetCode

lilo-thon authors no DSA teaching content of its own. Students are routed to NeetCode for both the explanation of a Topic and the Practice Problems on it, because NeetCode's explanations are already strong and the product's thesis is the bridge from academia to industry, not the teaching of data structures.

This narrows the "AI tutor" claim deliberately: the AI tutors at the hint and Project Checkpoint level, not at the level of explaining what an array is. It also creates the central constraint on Topic Completion — work done on NeetCode is invisible to the platform, so completion cannot be observed and must instead be established by the student bringing a solution back.
